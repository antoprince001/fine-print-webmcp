# Poster-image sources

These images are decorative background photography for the FinePrint hackathon demo. They were downloaded from Unsplash and cropped to a 2:3 poster format. The demo deliberately uses landscapes and avoids recognizable people, brands, and copyrighted artwork.

Unsplash grants a broad copyright license to download, modify, distribute, and use its images for free, including commercial use. Attribution is not required, but the source URLs are retained here for traceability. Review the current [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license) before reusing the assets outside this demo.
